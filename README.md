# 🌫️ Gas Sensor (MQ Series) – Air Quality & Safety Monitoring
## 📌 Overview
MQ series gas sensors are widely used for detecting gases such as LPG, methane, smoke, and carbon monoxide. These sensors play a critical role in safety systems, industrial monitoring, and smart home applications.
This repository focuses on the working mechanism, sensing characteristics, circuit design, and ESP32-based IoT integration of MQ sensors.
